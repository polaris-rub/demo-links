POLARIS

Provider Oriented Open Learning Analytics

POLARIS is an open-source Learning Analytics platform developed under the coordination of RWTH Aachen as part of the second funding phase of KI:edu.nrw. It combines learning data from multiple source systems and governs their use through privacy-compliant role and consent management. It is currently piloted at RWTH Aachen and Ruhr-Universität Bochum.
